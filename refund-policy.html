Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: April 2026

🛡️ We stand behind every CarGlanz product. If you are not satisfied or receive a damaged item, we will make it right — quickly and without hassle.

Return Eligibility

You may return a product within 7 days of delivery if it meets the following conditions:

  • The product is unopened and unused in its original packaging
  • The seal is intact and the product has not been tampered with
  • The return is initiated within 7 days of the delivery date

We are unable to accept returns for products that have been opened, used, or where the seal has been broken — unless the product is defective or damaged.

Defective or Damaged Products

If your product arrives damaged, leaking, or is defective in any way, we will provide a free replacement at no cost to you. Please:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of receiving the order
  • Share photos or a short video of the damaged product
  • We will arrange a replacement delivery within 3–5 business days

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Email us at support@carglanz.in with your order number and reason for return, or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Our team will confirm your return eligibility within 24 hours and share the return address.
  3. Pack the product securely in its original packaging and ship it to the address provided. Please share the courier tracking number with us.
  4. Once we receive and inspect the returned product, we will process your refund or replacement within 3–5 business days.

Refund Method and Timeline

  • Online payments (Card / UPI): Refund to original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery orders: Refund via bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS). Please share your bank account details when raising the return request.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Opened or used products (unless defective)
  • Products returned after 7 days of delivery
  • Products damaged due to misuse or improper application
  • Promotional / free gift items

Order Cancellation

You may cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it by contacting us at support@carglanz.in. Once the order is dispatched, cancellation is not possible — you may initiate a return after delivery instead.

Contact for Returns

  • Email: support@carglanz.in
  • WhatsApp: +91 9667692560 (Mon–Sat, 10am–6pm IST)

Please have your order number ready when contacting us — it helps us resolve your request faster.
